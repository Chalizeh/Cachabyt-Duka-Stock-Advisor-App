# Payment Verification and Order Confirmation

## Order statuses
1. **Awaiting payment** – order received; no stock is deducted.
2. **Confirmed** – seller checked the real payment account; stock is deducted.
3. **Ready for collection** – order has been prepared.
4. **Collected** – student has collected the order.
5. **Cancelled** – unpaid order cancelled.

## Seller steps
1. Select option 6 to view orders and order numbers.
2. Select option 7 to verify payment and confirm an order.
3. Check the actual M-Pesa/business account or other approved payment channel.
4. Enter payment method and transaction reference.
5. Confirm that you personally checked and received payment.
6. The app marks payment Verified, confirms the order and deducts stock.
7. Use option 9 to mark it Ready for collection and later Collected.

## If the buyer has not paid
1. Select option 8 and enter the order number.
2. The app creates and saves a reminder message.
3. Copy the reminder and send it to the buyer manually.
4. The order remains Awaiting payment; stock is not deducted.

## Safeguards
- A typed payment reference is recorded but is not automatic proof of payment.
- The prototype does not connect to M-Pesa or send SMS/WhatsApp automatically.
- Stock is deducted when payment is verified. Do not record the same order as another sale or stock will be deducted twice.
- Refunds for already-paid orders must be handled outside this prototype.
